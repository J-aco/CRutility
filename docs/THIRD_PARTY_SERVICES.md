# Third-party services and privacy

Settings, the remembered profile name/avatar URL, and rating matches are stored locally in the browser's extension storage on the users device. No data is sent back to the developer of this extension. There is no extension analytics or developer-operated backend. Opening external links is subject to the destination's policies.

Tenrai's [terms](https://tenrai.org/terms) permit application caching/display subject to third-party rights and technical limits. Attribution is appreciated rather than required by Tenrai.

With ratings enabled (the default), a title search is sent to api.tenrai.org when a local match is unavailable or expired; Tenrai also receives the requester's IP address and ordinary connection metadata. Profile details, credentials, and browsing history are not sent by this extension to Tenrai. Requests omit cookies and referrers. See [Tenrai's privacy policy](https://tenrai.org/privacy). Turning off "Show the MyAnimeList score" stops new lookups; an already sent request may finish.

MyAnimeList's [Terms of Use](https://myanimelist.net/about/terms_of_use) restrict content reuse and use of its marks. Tenrai does not grant rights in MAL content. Availability through Tenrai and attribution do not establish permission from MyAnimeList.
